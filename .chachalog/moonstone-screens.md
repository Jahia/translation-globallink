Redesigned the GlobalLink settings (Site settings) and the translation requests screen (jContent > Additional) with the jContent look: search, sorting, pages and CSV/JSON export of the requests, and a confirmation before old requests are deleted.

The module now requires jContent 3.7 or later. Before upgrading, check the jContent version in Administration > Modules: on an older jContent the module stays installed but does not start until jContent is upgraded.
